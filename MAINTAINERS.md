## Becoming a committer

We welcome new committers. The path:

1. **Sustained contribution** — ≥6 months of meaningful PRs across at least one area, ≥10 PRs merged.
2. **Code review participation** — providing thoughtful reviews on others' PRs in your area.
3. **Issue triage + community engagement** — answering questions on issues, discussions, or Discord.
4. **TSC nomination** — an existing committer or TSC member nominates you. The TSC votes (majority).
5. **Onboarding** — committer rights granted; you're added to this file; you're added to the relevant area's review-required list.

Committer rights are scoped to your primary area; cross-area merges still require a committer from that area to review. Committers can step back at any time; we move you to an "Emeritus committers" list (see below) and thank you for your work.

---

## Inactivity policy

Committers who have not contributed in **12 months** are moved to "Emeritus committers" with their consent. They're welcome to return and resume committer rights any time by opening an issue requesting reactivation; the TSC re-confirms.

This policy exists to keep the active committer list a true reflection of who's contributing now, without losing the historical record of who has shaped the project.
